## Ingredients

- 1 cup balsamic vinegar
- 1/4 cup brown sugar

## Directions

1. Combine the vinegar and the sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir to dissolve the sugar.
2. Simmer until reduced to about 1/3 to 1/4 cup and mixture becomes thick and syrupy.
3. Store in the fridge.
