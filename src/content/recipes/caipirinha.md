## Ingredients

- 1 lime, cut into 8 slices
- 2 shots cachaca
- 2 tsps sugar
- club soda

## Directions

1. Muddle 2 slices of lime with the sugar. Add several ice cubes.
2. Add cachaca. Top with club soda or fizzy mineral water to taste.
