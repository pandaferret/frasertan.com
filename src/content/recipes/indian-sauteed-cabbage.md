## Ingredients

- 1/2 head of green cabbage, sliced thinly
- 2tbspolive oil
- 1tspcumin seeds
- 1inchgingergrated
- 1tsppaprika
- 1tspcoriander
- 3/4cuppeascan use frozen or fresh peas
- saltto taste (roughly 1 tsp)

## Directions

1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds and ginger. Sauté for a minute and add cabbage, paprika, coriander powder, and salt.
2. Mix well and add 1/4 cup water.
3. Cover the pan and cook for 8-10 minutes until the cabbage is fork tender.
4. Add in the green peas and cook for another minute or too. Serve hot.
