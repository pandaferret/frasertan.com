## Ingredients

- 1 peach, split in half, pit removed
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- honey
- heavy cream

## Directions

1. Place each peach half in a snug fitting ramekin. Sprinkle with vanilla and drizzle with honey. Sprinkle with brown sugar.
2. Bake in a 350°F oven for 20-30 minutes.
3. Pour in a little cream if wanted and eat!
