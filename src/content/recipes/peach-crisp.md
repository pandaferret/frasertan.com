## Ingredients

Filling:

- 5 cups sliced peaches, fresh or thawed from frozen, drained and patted dry
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/8 tsp. salt
- 1 T. lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp. vanilla

Topping:

- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar, light or dark
- 2/3 cup flour
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cut into cubes
- 2/3 cup whole or quick oats

Place on top:

- 2/3 cup chopped or halved pecans

## Equipment

- 9 x 13 inch baking dish

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the peaches with the other filling ingredients.
3. For the topping, mix together the brown sugar, flour, cinnamon and salt, then mix in the butter and oats.
4. Place the peach mixture in a 9 x 13 inch baking dish and cover with the topping. Spread the pecans on top.
5. Bake for 45-50 minutes. Enjoy warm with vanilla ice cream.
