## Ingredients

- 1/2 red onion, chopped fine
- 3-4 scallions, chopped
- 2 cups orzo
- 1 tbsp butter
- salt
- pepper

## Directions

1. Start a pot of water boiling. Meanwhile, saute the onion and scallion in a bit of oil until softened. Season with a pinch of salt.

2. Cook orzo until al dente and drain.

3. Add orzo, butter, onions and scallions to a bowl and toss to mix, melting the butter. Season with salt and pepper.
