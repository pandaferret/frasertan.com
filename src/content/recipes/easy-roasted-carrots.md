## Ingredients

- 1 lb carrots
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Pepper
- Maple syrup (optional)

## Equipment

- Rimmed baking sheet

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Peel the carrots. Remove the ends, and halve any large ones.
3. On a rimmed baking sheet, toss the carrots with olive oil, salt and pepper to taste.
4. Roast for 20 minutes until tender (bigger carrots may take longer).
5. Remove and immediately drizzle with maple syrup, if using. Serve.
