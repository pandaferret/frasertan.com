## Ingredients

- 0.5 c (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3 cups powdered sugar, sifted
- 4 tbsp heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Cream butter in the bowl of a stand mixer with the whisk until light and fluffy. Slowly beat in powdered sugar in small batches - it will fly everywhere!
2. Add cream and vanilla and beat on high until light and fluffy.
3. Optional: Replace the whisk with the paddle attachment and beat for a few seconds to get rid of air bubbles.
