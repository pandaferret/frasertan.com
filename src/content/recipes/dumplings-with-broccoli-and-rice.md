## Ingredients

- Frozen dumplings
- Broccoli
- Rice

## Directions

1. Make the rice in the rice cooker.
2. Cook the broccoli like so: [The Best Way to Cook Broccoli](/recipes/the-best-way-to-cook-broccoli/).
3. Pan fry the dumplings.
4. Devour.
