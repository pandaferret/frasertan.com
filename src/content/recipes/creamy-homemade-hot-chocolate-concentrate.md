## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup white sugar, or to taste
- 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 1/3 cup boiling water
- 3/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 to 1 cup cream
- Milk, for serving

## Directions

1. In a small saucepan, combine the sugar, cocoa powder and salt. Whisk in the boiling water. Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer, stirring constantly to prevent scorching. Simmer for 2 minutes.
2. Off heat, stir in the vanilla and the cream.
3. To make hot cocoa, either:
   - Return the pan to the stove and add 3 1/2 cups milk. Warm gently on low until it reaches the desired temp.
   - Pour some of the concentrate into a mug and add milk to taste, then warm in the microwave.
   - Warm the concentrate and drink it directly, because you are worth it. It's highly reminiscent of rich, bitter European-style hot chocolate.
