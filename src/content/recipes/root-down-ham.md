## Ingredients

- 1 Root Down smoked ham, defrosted
- 1 cup orange juice
- 1 cup brown sugar

## Equipment

- Sous vide (optional)
- Roasting pan with rack (or cookie rack and cookie sheet)

## Directions

1. Cook the ham in a sous vide cooker set to 140°F for at least 3 and up to 8 hours. Remove the ham from the sous vide and place on a rack set in a roasting pan.
2. Meanwhile, combine the orange juice and the sugar in a small saucepan and simmer over medium heat until thickened.
3. Preheat oven to 500°F 30 minutes prior to serving the ham. Place the ham in the oven and roast for 5 minutes. Then, pull out the rack and baste the ham all over with the glaze. Roast an additional 5 minutes, then repeat glazing. Repeat an additional time.
4. Remove the ham from the oven and let rest 5 minutes. Slice and serve.

## Notes

- Kenji originally calls for balsamic vinegar instead of orange juice - see [Baked Ham](/recipes/baked-ham/).
- You can also roast the ham from defrosted to warm it up instead of using the sous vide: bake at 325°F for 16-20 minutes per pound, glazing and basting as you go.
