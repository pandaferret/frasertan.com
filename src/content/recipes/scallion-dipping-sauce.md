## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons mirin
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 teaspoon chili oil, (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 medium scallion, white and green parts, minced

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in bowl and serve.
