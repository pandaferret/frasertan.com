## Ingredients

- 4 cups cherry tomatoes
- 8 oz burrata cheese
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup pine nuts
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chiffonade
- Balsamic glaze
- Crostini or your choice of bread, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a baking dish, add the tomatoes, 2 tbsp of the olive oil, garlic, 2 tbsp of the pine nuts, salt, black pepper, and red chili flakes. Mix all ingredients, and place in the oven for 30 minutes.
3. After 30 minutes, remove the baking dish from the oven and allow the tomatoes to cool a little, then add the fresh basil and mix.
4. Transfer the tomatoes to a bowl, top with the burrata, drizzle with the remaining olive oil and balsamic glaze, and top with more basil, pine nuts, salt and black pepper.
5. Enjoy with crostini or your choice of bread.
