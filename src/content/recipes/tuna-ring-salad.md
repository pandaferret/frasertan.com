## Ingredients

- 2 cans chunk light tuna
- 5 ounces small sized pasta, cooked (this appears to be a regional thing. Some places don’t sell small rings, so use small shells)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 cup chopped celery
- 5 hard boiled eggs, cut up
- Juice of one lemon

Dressing:

- 6 or so overfilled tablespoons of mayonnaise, to taste (I usually use lite)
- Lots of fresh pepper
- Lawry’s or other seasoned salt
- Garlic powder to taste

## Directions

1. Drain tuna and mix with juice of one lemon.
2. Mix everything together and add dressing.
3. Refrigerate until time to eat.
